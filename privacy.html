Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

GSE is a game in which a private group of friends runs its own fictional stock exchange. This policy explains what GSE collects, who can see it, and how to delete it. In short: we collect only what the game needs to work, we don't track you or show ads, and your market is visible only to its members.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and the display name you choose. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address (or an Apple private relay address, if you choose to hide yours) and, where you allow it, your name. We don't receive your Apple or Google password.
  • An account ID that links your account to your markets.
  • What you do in the game. The markets you open or join and your role in them; the securities you list, including a friend's name, a ticker, and a company name; the reports you file, including the description you write, the direction, and the severity; trading halts; and reports you flag for review.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to operate and secure the service.

On your iPhone, the app also stores a few settings that never leave the device, such as the last market you viewed and which market you use in each Messages conversation.

We don't collect your contacts, location, photos, or messages. The Messages extension can't read your conversations; it only places an alert you choose to send.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to sign you in, show your markets to their members, calculate prices and charts, attribute reports ("FILED BY"), keep the game fair (for example, limits on how often you can report), and handle flagged content. We also count, in aggregate, how markets are used (for example, how many reports a market files each week) to decide how to improve the game; those counts don't include names or what reports say. We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see it

  • Members of your market see its securities, prices, reports, halts, and members, including your display name and the reports you file. Anyone who later joins the market also sees its history. People who leave a market remain named on reports they filed there.
  • People in a conversation where you send a market alert see that alert's text. Alerts you send can't be edited or withdrawn by GSE.
  • Nobody else. Markets are private; there are no public profiles, feeds, or rankings.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in, on servers in the United States.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose them.
  • Apple TestFlight distributes the beta. If you allow it, TestFlight sends us crash reports and the feedback you submit, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: on the Markets screen, tap the person icon to open your account, then tap DELETE ACCOUNT. When you do:

  • Your email address, display name, and account are deleted.
  • Markets you own pass to another member, and markets where you were the only member are deleted.
  • Reports you filed stay in their markets without your name ("FILED BY FORMER MEMBER"), because other members' prices depend on them.

A market's owner or admins can remove the text of a report; removed text is deleted, not hidden. To ask for anything else to be removed, email us.

Children

GSE is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your information. You can change your display name and delete your account in the app; for anything else, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app or through TestFlight.
