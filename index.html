A private stock exchange for your friend group.

Every friend is a listed security. When something happens, anyone in the group files a market report, and the price moves: up for bringing snacks, down for "on my way" from the shower.

MIKE ▲ 12.4%

Shares of MIKE rose 12.4% following reports that he bought the first round.

How it works

  • Open a private market and invite your group with a code or a link.
  • List your friends. Each one opens at $100.00.
  • Report what happens, from the app or right inside iMessage. The market moves, the charts update, and the alert lands in the chat.

Beta

GSE is in a private beta on iPhone through TestFlight. It's a game: prices are fictional, and there is no real money, trading, or investing of any kind.
